# Quantum Computing Course: Discrete Variable & Continuous Variable

## From Beginner to Advanced

Welcome to this comprehensive course on Quantum Computing, covering both **Discrete Variable (DV)** and **Continuous Variable (CV)** paradigms. This course takes you from fundamental concepts to cutting-edge research topics.

---

## Course Overview

| Level | Modules | Focus Areas |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Beginner** | 1-4 | Foundations, Math, DV/CV Introduction |
| **Medium** | 5-8 | Gates, Operations, Algorithms, Photonics |
| **Advanced** | 9-12 | Error Correction, Hybrid Systems, QML, Research |

---

## What You'll Learn

### Discrete Variable (DV) Quantum Computing
- Qubit-based systems
- Quantum gates and circuits
- Superconducting and trapped-ion implementations
- Standard quantum algorithms (Shor's, Grover's, VQE)

### Continuous Variable (CV) Quantum Computing
- Bosonic modes and quadrature operators
- Gaussian states and operations
- Photonic quantum computing
- GKP encoding and cat qubits

### Mathematical Foundations
- Linear algebra for quantum mechanics
- Hilbert spaces and operators
- Density matrices and quantum channels
- Phase space representations
